Build a road. Break a blockade. Turn a stack into your next move.

Stones is an abstract strategy board game inspired by Tak. Start with classic two-player Square play, then explore Stones variants for two to four players on Square or Hex boards.

Place flat stones to connect opposite sides, raise walls to block a route, and use capstones to flatten walls. Pick up and spread stacks to change who controls the board. A quiet position can become a race for a road in a single turn.

PLAY YOUR WAY

• Challenge computer opponents from Easy to Expert.
• Pass and play with friends on one device.
• Invite friends online with room codes and links.
• Mix local players, online friends and AI at the same table.
• Customize board size, seats, turn order and optional clocks.

LEARN, PRACTICE AND EXPLORE

• Learn through guided Square and Hex tutorials.
• Solve tactical puzzles and practice road building, blocking and stack movement.
• Try Court practice for coaching and room to explore.
• Inspect every layer of a stack before planning a move.
• Save and resume local games, then review completed games move by move.

MAKE THE TABLE YOURS

Explore five coordinated board and piece styles: Classic Wood, Slate, Marble, Morocco and Kyoto. Earn achievements and unlock cosmetics through play. Adjust appearance, sound and music to suit your table.

FIND YOUR STANDING

Follow human leaderboard rankings and your profile standing. Google sign-in is optional for local play and helps you return to your online rooms with the same account.

Local and computer games can be played offline. Online play, account services and leaderboards require an internet connection. Achievements, cosmetic unlocks and preferences are stored on your device; signing in does not synchronize them across devices.

Square games with more than two players and Hex games use Stones variant rules. Classic Tak is two-player Square.
