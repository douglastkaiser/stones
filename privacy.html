Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 18, 2026

Overview

Your privacy matters. You can play locally without signing in. Online play, Google sign-in, ratings and voluntary support email use the information described below.

This Privacy Policy explains how Stones ("the App"), developed by Douglas T Kaiser ("we", "us", or "our"), handles your information.

Data We Collect

Stones uses information needed to operate the features you choose:

  • Accounts: Firebase Authentication manages guest and Google identities. A signed-in Google profile may include your user ID, display name and email address.
  • Online games: Firebase stores room membership, configuration, moves and related game state so players can return to their games.
  • Player names: your chosen Stones name is public by player ID, separate from your Google profile. You can edit it from your home-page player profile.
  • Open tables: if a host chooses Open to anyone, the room invitation, host ID and board/player-count summary become discoverable by other players. Removing a listing hides it from discovery; existing invitations remain usable. Expired listings are hidden, but this does not automatically delete the stored summary.
  • Community ratings: standings and display aliases are public; result records and supporting evidence are stored to process ratings and prevent duplicate results.
  • Website analytics: the web version initializes Google Analytics through Firebase, which may use cookies or similar browser storage and collect usage and device information under Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Play Games Services

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games Services to access features like leaderboards and achievements, your interaction with those services is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Play Games is separate from your Stones account and may provide profile, achievement, leaderboard and legacy cloud-save features when configured.

Online Multiplayer

Online rooms and their recorded moves are stored in Firebase. Room codes and membership help control access. Share invitation codes only with people you want to invite.

Game and rating records can remain after a game finishes; Stones does not currently promise automatic deletion when a game ends. Signing out does not delete these records.

Local Data Storage

The App may store the following data locally on your device:

  • Game settings and preferences
  • Current game state and room shortcuts (for resuming games)
  • Learning completions, achievements and earned cosmetics

Local play and progress are saved on the device. Online game state is sent to Firebase when you play online; the optional Play Games integration may also save compatible legacy games to Google. Earned Stones progress is not currently synchronized across devices by your Stones account.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Games Services (optional) - For leaderboards and achievements. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase / Google - For account authentication, online game storage, community ratings and website analytics.
  • Your email provider - When you choose to send a feedback or support email.

Feedback and Support

Send feedback in Settings or About opens an email draft addressed to douglastkaiser@gmail.com. Nothing is sent until you send the email in your email app. We receive your email address and the content you choose to send so we can investigate issues and respond.

You can review or omit the optional app version and platform details. Stones does not automatically attach account IDs, room codes, logs or game histories. Copy report places the report on your clipboard. Stones does not save the feedback form as a draft.

Do not include passwords, invitation codes or private game content. Support correspondence may remain in the support mailbox; contact us if you wish to request removal.
